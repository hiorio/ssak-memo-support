Privacy Policy

Effective August 29, 2026

1. Core principle

Bud Memo requires no account and uses no developer-operated server, advertising, or analytics tools. The developer does not collect, store, or track personal information or note content through the app.

2. Information stored on your device

Notes, recognized dictation text, tags, favorite status, and temporary drafts are stored on your device. Daily Markdown files are created in the app’s document area, which you can access in Files.

3. Microphone and speech recognition

Microphone and speech-recognition access is requested only when you choose a voice note. On supported devices, Bud Memo uses Apple’s on-device speech recognition for Korean, English, or Japanese according to the language selected in the app. Original audio is neither created as a file nor stored. You can review and save only the recognized text.

4. External transmission and third parties

Bud Memo does not send notes, audio, identifiers, usage history, or diagnostics to the developer. It includes no advertising network or third-party analytics SDK. If data is synchronized by operating-system backup or a file provider you select, the policies and settings of that Apple ID or service apply.

5. Retention and deletion

Notes remain on your device until you delete them. You can permanently delete notes from Trash. Deleting the app removes data in its local container. Copies left separately in Files or backups must be removed from those locations by you.

6. Changing permissions

You can change microphone and speech-recognition access at any time in iPhone Settings. Typing and Library features remain available if you decline these permissions.

7. Policy changes

If features or data practices change, this policy and its effective date will be updated. Important changes will be announced in release notes or on the support page.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, use the Bud Memo support channel.
