Bud Memo is an iPhone app for quickly recording or typing from a widget or Control Center while keeping your notes on your device.

Frequently asked questions

Where are my notes stored?
Notes are stored in the app’s local library. Daily Markdown files are available in Files under “On My iPhone > Bud Memo.”

Are audio recordings saved?
No. On supported devices, speech is converted to text in Korean, English, or Japanese according to the selected app language. Original audio is neither created as a file nor retained.

How do I delete a note completely?
Move it to Trash from the Library, then delete it permanently. Deleting the app also removes data in its local container. Any Markdown files left separately in Files can be deleted there.
